# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Conatus contributors commit to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be
constructive, assume good faith while evaluating technical claims critically,
and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another
person's private information, or retaliation for a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this standard.
Reports should be sent privately to the conduct contact listed in the repository's
security contact mechanism. Until that private address is published, contact the
repository owner privately rather than opening a public issue.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to assess and respond to a
report, avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome when appropriate.
Knowingly false reports may themselves violate this code; an unsubstantiated
good-faith report does not.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. It does not replace applicable law or platform terms.
